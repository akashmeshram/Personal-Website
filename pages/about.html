Hello! I'm Akash Meshram.

A graduate from IIT Kharagpur interested in interdisciplinary technologies and lover of color blue. I've experience in Software Development, Machine Learning and Materials Science.

Passionate about creating better human experiences and figuring out how technology can simplify lives, my work focuses on public and socially engaged products.

As a son of a government servant I got the chance to live all over India, explore diverse backgrounds and interact with distinct communities. I draw inspiration from their multilayered perspective and unheard narratives.

In my free time, I enjoy studying history, travelling and consuming samosas.
