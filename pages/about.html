Hey there! I'm, Akash

I'm a grad from IIT Kharagpur and lover of color blue. I work across software engineering, applied AI, and human-centered design.

Most of my work has focused on tools that sit at the intersection of technology, human behavior, and real-world constraints, whether that's simplifying workflows, improving decision-making, or making complex systems easier to interact with.

Because my parent worked in government service, I grew up moving across different parts of India. Living in multiple regions and cultures shaped how I think about people, institutions, and scale, and it continues to influence how I approach design and technology.

Outside of work, I enjoy reading history, traveling to new places, and finding the perfect samosa.

Always happy to chat about tech, collaborate on interesting problems, or just nerd out over ideas — feel free to reach out.
