I'm Akash a graduate from IIT Kharagpur interested in interdisciplinary technologies and lover of color blue. I've experiences in software engineering, applied AI, and human computer interaction.

Passionate about creating better human experiences and figuring out how technology can simplify everyday life. My work focuses on public and socially engaged products that create a positive impact on communities.

Growing up as a son of government servant I had the privilege to live all over India, explore diverse backgrounds and interact with distinct communities. These encounters have profoundly shaped my perspective on life. I draw inspiration from their multilayered perspective and unheard narratives.

In my free time, I enjoy reading history, travelling and consuming samosas. Always happy to chat about tech, collaborate on interesting problems, or just nerd out over ideas, feel free to reach out.
